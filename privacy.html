Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the YT Automation Ops production tool ("the app") handles information when its owner signs in with a Google account.

Who uses the app

The app is a private tool used only by its owner for the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not available to the public and does not collect information from other people.

Google data the app accesses

  • YouTube (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube): to upload videos and Shorts to the owner's own channels, set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, and check their processing status.
  • Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): to create folders and upload production files (images, audio, JSON scripts) to the owner's Drive. With this permission the app can only see and change files it created itself; it cannot access any other files in Drive.

How the data is used and stored

Google data is used only to perform the actions above for the owner. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer or server and are never sent to third parties. The app does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or for training AI models.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the locally stored token files removes the app's access data. Videos and Drive files created by the app belong to the account owner and can be deleted from YouTube or Drive at any time.
