YT Automation Ops

YT Automation Ops is a private production tool used by its owner to create and publish documentary-style videos on the owner's own YouTube channels (for example, Through Time Files, published in multiple languages).

The tool prepares videos, uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channels, and stores the production files (images, narration audio and scripts) in the owner's own Google Drive.

The tool is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.
